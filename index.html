4WD obstacle-avoiding robot

A four-wheel car that drives by itself. Ultrasonic “eyes” on a servo “neck” watch what is ahead and turn to check left and right, and an Arduino tells an L298N motor driver where to drive. Build it step by step in 3D, then learn how every part works.

For grown-ups: exact pins
Wiring

Every connection, and why

Wire colours match the 3D builder. Red is always + power, black is always GND (ground).

Complete wiring diagram

Swipe sideways to see the whole diagram.

Both left motors share OUT1/OUT2 and both right motors share OUT3/OUT4. Every “+” goes to the breadboard red row, every “GND” to the blue row.

Battery red goes to the switch’s middle leg, then 12V. Battery black goes to GND. Swapping them shorts the battery: sparks and heat.
